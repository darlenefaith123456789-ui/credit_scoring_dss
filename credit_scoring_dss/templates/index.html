Credit Scoring Decision Support System

Decide on installment loan applications faster and more consistently.

Enter an applicant's details and the loan they are asking for. The system checks them against what it learned from past financing records and tells you how risky the applicant is, what it recommends, and why.

Assess an applicant

What the result means

  • Low risk Likely to pay on time. Approve
  • Medium risk Some warning signs. Look closer. Review
  • High risk Similar to past borrowers who defaulted. Reject
